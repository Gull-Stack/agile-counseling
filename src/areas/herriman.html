Getting to Agile Counseling from Herriman

Our office is at 45 W 9000 S, Suite 1, in Sandy, just west of State Street and a few blocks south of the 9000 South exit on I-15. From Herriman, most clients take Mountain View Corridor or Bangerter Highway north and then 9000 South east, or 11400 South to I-15 and north one exit. Plan for roughly 20 to 30 minutes in normal traffic. Parking is free, and the office is wheelchair accessible.

If the drive is the obstacle, telehealth is a full alternative. Many Herriman clients begin with an in-person first session and then alternate with secure video sessions from home. Evening traffic on Bangerter is the usual reason people switch.
